# Code of conduct

mori is a small project. The bar is ordinary respect.

- Assume good intent, and be specific when something is wrong.
- Critique the work. Leave the person out of it.
- No harassment, slurs, or sexual attention that was not asked for.
- Do not publish someone else's private context. mori repos can hold notes, chats, and passwords.

If a conversation goes bad, stop, and write to the maintainers through the security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if it should not be public. Maintainers may lock a thread or decline a contribution that breaks this.
